## Design Context

### Users
CEO and business stakeholders — executive audience making high-level decisions about livestock operations across multiple farms. Non-technical users who need at-a-glance clarity on KPIs, trends, and anomalies. Used primarily at a desk but must also work on tablets/phones for on-the-go reviews.

### Brand Personality
Modern, clean, efficient. Data should feel authoritative and trustworthy. The interface should convey operational precision — like a control room, not a consumer app.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode, data-dense dashboard. Teal accent (#14F1D9) as the primary brand color. The design should feel like a premium operations tool — tight spacing, clear hierarchy, no decorative noise. Anti-references: none specified, but avoid consumer/marketing aesthetics, glassmorphism, and anything that feels playful or casual.

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over decoration** — every visual element must earn its place by aiding comprehension
2. **Hierarchy through contrast** — data values > labels > supporting text, enforced through size/weight/color
3. **Responsive without compromise** — mobile must be a first-class experience, not a shrunk desktop
4. **Consistent but not monotonous** — shared patterns across all 12 tabs, with enough variation to orient users quickly
5. **Efficiency for repeat users** — CEOs return daily; navigation, scanning, and filtering should be fast and habitual
