Checks

passed

Invalid payment amount is blocked by the real form

The drawer was opened in Chrome and a zero-amount payment was submitted. Native validation stopped the submit and the database stayed at one payment for INR 1,000.

passed

Existing payment amount and note can be edited

The first receipt was changed to INR 1,500 with a corrected note. The payment row and sale received/balance totals updated transactionally.

passed

Payment note can be cleared

The same receipt was edited again to INR 1,750 with a blank note. The stored note became empty text, proving optional text edits are not skipped.

passed

Additional payment can be added

A second receipt was added from the drawer. The database showed two payments and total received of INR 2,000.

passed

Any payment can be removed, including the final receipt

The second receipt was deleted, then the final receipt was deleted. The sale returned to INR 0 received and INR 10,000 balance with no stale payment rows.

passed

Sale tagging records the actual male/female counts

Two male animals were selected from the Tag animals drawer and confirmed sold. The deal row stored male_count=2, female_count=0, and both goats moved to sold.

passed

Sold animals leave the candidate picker

After confirmation, the tag drawer was reopened and the sold animals no longer appeared as sellable candidates.

passed

Backend contract drives controls and copy

The UI uses backend-exposed controls for add, update, delete, and sale tagging actions, including separate unavailable versus empty picker copy.
