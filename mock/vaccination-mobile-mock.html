    What the colours mean
    Red · Missed — the dose window closed and the animal wasn't recovered into a compatible drive in time; leadership was alerted ahead. Amber · In buffer — overdue but still recoverable by reschedule into a compatible drive, no dose missed. In/out-of-buffer is computed by backend policy (policySnapshot) — cycle-relative for missed doses, plus the recovery re-entry window; the app only renders the label.
