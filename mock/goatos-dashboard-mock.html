Supplier warmup — Holding Farm (pre-arrival)

journey starts at purchase
A purchased goat’s journey starts at the place of purchase, not on arrival — tagged + vaccinated at the supplier’s Holding Farm, ownership shared / pending (advance paid, balance on dispatch). Warmup length depends on purpose & animal: breeding stock 45–70 days (realistic today); fattening / sheep 0–2 weeks, sometimes shipped same-day. (2–8 weeks = optimistic future target.) HF doses import as completion evidence so post-arrival obligations never double-dose. A goat can be rejected before it is loaded on the truck: rejects never ship, never enter the park count, and raise a supplier credit.
