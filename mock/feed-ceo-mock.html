What is removed for the CEO

  • The 74-row shed-session list. Pending / Experiment chips are a worklist. A CEO cannot act on “Castro 1 · Morning · Pending” seventy times.
  • Partition rows. Sumathi 1 – Part 1…8 becomes one shed row with a time span. 74 rows → 35, and the default view shows 4.
  • Filter panel. Five dropdowns become three: date, session, park. Feed type and status are answers, not inputs.
  • “Confirm each shed-session was fed.” That is the operator’s instruction. The CEO is not confirming anything.

Tab 1 · Feeding — water timing is the number

The sheet you were sent by hand is a list of 74 start times. Rendered as a clock it answers in one glance what the list never did:

  • Did the round close before the deadline, and by how much? 12:26:03 against 12:30 — under 4 minutes of margin.
  • Is the margin trending to zero? Seven-day bars. 13 Aug already breached; nothing in the old sheet showed that.
  • Who waited longest? Godel 1 drank 3 h 25 m after Gandhi 1. Same ration, very different day for the animal.
  • Where did the time go? A 55-minute hole between Ho Chi Minh and Mandela is the entire margin.
  • Who is missing entirely? 3 pens in the sheet have no water line at all — invisible in a list, red on a clock.

Tab 2 · Packing — was the right quantity packed, in time

Packing is one video per bag, per pen, per session — so raw coverage is a big number that is always near 100% and tells the CEO nothing. What matters:

  • Packed on a stale count. Animals shifted after the 13:30 diff cutoff means bags weighed for a head count that no longer exists. That is real feed lost, and it is the only packing number with money attached.
  • Filmed but unverified. Different failure from unpacked. Feed already went out; the proof is just unjudged.
  • Re-shoot loops. A bag rejected twice is a training problem, not a packing problem.
  • Cutoff margin, not a completion tick: 14:52 against a 15:00 staging cutoff.

Tab 3 · Transport — one clip per shed, no grace

Only 35 loads exist, so a list is survivable — but still the wrong frame. The CEO frame is breach and repeat:

  • Loads staged by 15:00, with the late ones named, timed, and attributed.
  • Repeat breaches over 7 days. One shed three times is a route problem; three sheds once each is weather.
  • The downstream link. Late staging on Day N shows up as late water on Day N+1 — sheds staged late started 68 min later. This is the single most useful thing the three tabs can say together, and neither tab can say it alone.

Shown as disabled, with the reason

  • Delivered kg vs planned kg. The weight proof is a photo of a scale. Nothing reads the number, so a variance figure would be invented. Needs a connected scale or a verifier-entered reading.
  • Feed stock burn and shortage risk. Packing does not read an inventory ledger.
  • Vehicle, route, trip cost. A transport task carries shed + operator + video. No vehicle, no route.

Each is drawn as a dashed, dimmed card stating what is missing — never a plausible-looking number with no source behind it.

The chain strip

Present on all three tabs: Packed → Staged → Water, each with its actual time and its deadline. Feed is one chain across two days; the tabs are three windows onto it. The strip is also the navigation — tapping a link opens that tab already scoped to the same date and park.
