## Configuration and execution ownership synthesis

1. **Master records and vocabulary:** inventory, feed items, locations, people, vendor/market choices and protocol categories have separate identities and validators. A central authoring interface must preserve them; category/module applicability is new metadata, not replacement authorization.
2. **Authored operational configuration:** feed ration/schedule dimensions and effective dates, weighing published capture/planning rules and separate calendar DB settings, clinical protocols, vaccination capacity and rules, market call time, known Alerts settings. A generic scalar is insufficient where the domain stores a matrix/version/effective period.
3. **Derived/read-only values:** Work Board, counts/weights/feed/sales analytics, stock positions, realized prices and clinical recommendations depend on operational records. Do not seed editable copies of those outputs as policy.
4. **Procedure authoring:** SOP definition/version, form/proof compatibility, registered task types and follow-up tracks already exist. Actual nested child-SOP identity/version/subject mapping remains proposed; requires/after_step primitives do not prove arbitrary nesting.
5. **Runtime execution:** tasks, obligations, verification, proof, stock, canonical identity/location and outbox own effects. Starting an instruction or checking a local box cannot bypass their writes, approvals, idempotency or conflicts.

## Authorization, approvals and sync contracts

`backend/internal/permissions/routes.go:5–17` distinguishes required-all and required-any capabilities. Read the indexed route entry for each write; role names alone are not authorization. Examples: sale allocation uses SalesAllocateAnimals rather than generic SalesWrite (`:35–44`); animal-purchase decision form is backend-capability gated; Alerts configure follows per-person HRMS ticks/migration321.
