# CEO clarity constraint — current design

Configuration has two jobs: **Items and settings** and **Work instructions**. Choose a department to open its existing SOPs. Inside an SOP, combine smaller SOPs using **Stages and approvals**; each stage explains what must happen first, who approves it, and any waiting period. A child SOP is an actual saved procedure with a pinned version, not a typed name.

The previously proposed Connect related work, Workflow links and Run insights destinations are superseded and removed from navigation. Their old URLs open Work instructions. Procurement is an optional worked example, never the definition of generic configuration.

Preserve existing production menus, submenus, accordion behaviour, labels, buttons, typography and dark-green visual language. Existing department settings retain ownership: vaccine schedules stay in Vaccination, treatment protocols in Health, sales eligibility in Sales. Shared items and values can be used by those features without flattening their specialised rules.

Every visible action must explain its business outcome. Advanced libraries and evidence are secondary. The local prototype boundary stays clear. No production tasks, records, clinical schedules or approvals are changed by practice.

Judges must test the actual screens and negative paths, not approve from screenshots or wording alone. Required lenses and the complete source ledger are in requirements-consolidated.md and feature-requirement-matrix.md. Earlier judge receipts describe superseded iterations and do not certify this design.
