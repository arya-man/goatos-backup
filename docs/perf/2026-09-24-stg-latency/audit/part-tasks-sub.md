### Notes
- **No non-PASS endpoints.** Planning time is 60-90% of DB time on every endpoint because the joins and views are large (getRow plans in 9ms and executes in 0.4ms). A pgx statement cache (QueryExecModeCacheStatement) removes most of the planning cost, so check the pool's exec mode.
- **The main latency risk is round trips, not the DB.** The SOP task detail makes 3 + S sequential queries. Submit makes about 6 + 2(1 + S) + 2. Batch or aggregate these.
- **obligation_instances:** no GET in this scope touches it. The POSTs reach it only through indexed paths: the PK (scan-captures) and (tenant_id, batch_id, status) (submit's needs_review check, 786 rows probed, 2.9ms). The attempt CTE joins on obligation_id (PK). None of these endpoints can seq-scan it, so the clone's 26k seq scans come from other modules. Likely sources are the calendar and vaccination-drive queries that filter by status or due_at without batch_id IS NULL, or by target_type='goat' outside the partial predicates. Look at those in the calendar/obligations part.
