1. Where a vaccine rule lives

Take one rule: "ET+TT booster, 3 weeks after the first dose".

Today

The same rule is written in 4 places. Code keeps them in sync on every publish.

protocol_definitionsThe "Vaccination" family. One row, holds the versions below.
↓
protocol_versions.rule_dslV1, V2 … V10. Each version is a big JSON blob of the whole plan, and ET+TT's 3 weeks sits inside it.
↓ publish copies it into
protocol_rulesOne row per dose (et_tt_kid_4w, et_tt_adult_w2 …), created again for every version.
↓ and into
protocol_rule_dimensionsThe same rule's filters (species, stage, purpose …) copied out so they can be searched.
↓ and tracked by
protocol_rule_lineage"V10's et_tt_adult_w2 is the same rule as V9's", so open tasks aren't orphaned.
+ vaccination_anchor_events, vaccination_capacity_configMore copies of parts of the same JSON.

New

Written once. No versions, so nothing to copy or link across.

vaccinesOne row: ET+TT, killed, 2-week gap, can share a day.
↓
program_dosesOne row: program "Farm-born", ET+TT, from birth, 4 weeks, booster +3 weeks, repeat 26 weeks.
config_change_logA plain record: "Ravi changed booster 3→4 weeks on 24 Sep". It's history only; the scheduler never reads it.

3. How an animal gets its due dates

Today: three different ways, depending on the animal

A

Kid (farm-born): birth_age rules. Days from date of birth.

B

Bought adult: procurement_policy waves (first wave, second wave after N days, separate goat and sheep waves, breeding/fattening plans).

C

Anything else: manual_campaign rules or anchors, meaning a base date typed in by hand that shifts or suppresses doses.Anchors alone took 25+ fix commits: suppressing first doses, boosters counting from the wrong dose, future or missed anchors

+

Kid or adult? Decided by a cutoff age (kids_normal_schedule_until_weeks), which then decides which of A, B or C applies.

New: one way for everyone

1

Which programs does this animal match? This comes from the checkboxes: origin, species, gender, tags, breed, age.

2

For each dose: due = starting date + weeks. The starting date is always a fact we already store:
birth · arrival at buying spot · arrival at farm · previous dose

3

Skip doses already given. Buying-spot doses count.

4

Push the date later if the animal is sick, pregnant (months 4–5), just arrived, or too close to another vaccine.

✓

No anchors, no waves, no campaigns, and no kid/adult switch. A bought adult is just "counted from arrival".

4. From due dates to work on the board

Today

1

The sweeper runs every 5 minutes, once per protocol version, in priority order.10 SweepVersion* variants · separate park_consolidation.go (1,321 lines) that repeats the date logic

2

A preflight step (627 lines) re-simulates the sweep to settle the per-visit shot cap.

3

Drives, tasks and stock reservations are made. Another pass then marks doses missed.Bugs fixed here: phantom "missed" for animals already dosed; work dropped on blocked dates or races; pen/park scope leaks

New: the same job, one code path

1

Every 5 minutes, take all due doses for a pen (or a park, same function) that fall inside their window.

2

Pick the drive day that batches the most doses safely. Keep to 3 vaccines per animal per day (high priority first) and 200 animals per operator per day.

3

Anything over a limit moves to the next safe day, never dropped. Reserve vials, and flag it if stock is short.

4

Past "late by" → missed → Preventive Care review. Dosed and waiting for proof counts as done.

5. What stays the same

The Android capture app, drives, the work board, proof and verification, inventory lots and stock movements, and the obligation_instances table (renamed to due doses). The change is only in how rules are stored and how due dates are worked out.
