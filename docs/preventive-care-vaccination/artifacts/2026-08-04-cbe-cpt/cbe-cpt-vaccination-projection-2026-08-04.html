How each date is derived

Which projections the approved schedule can work out on its own, and which are dates the farm team asked for. ET+TT throughout this plan is system-calculated. The adult Blue Tongue second dose is not.

Projection itemBasisHow to use it here
ET+TT kid first dose System rule 4 weeks (28 days) from date of birth, or from a trusted assumed age
ET+TT kid booster System rule 7 weeks (49 days) from date of birth, and never less than 21 days after the first dose
ET+TT adult second dose System rule 21 days after the previous accepted dose, with a 21-day minimum gap
ET+TT repeat after a completed course System rule 182 days after the completed course
Newborn 901007000504378 System rule Born 2026-07-25 plus 21 days for the first field action, then the booster 21 days after the dose is actually given — 15 Aug, then 5 Sep
Pox and Blue Tongue after a recent ET+TT System rule A 14-day safety gap applies unless an approved same-day pair is chosen. This is what supports the CPT "from 10 Aug onward" rows after the 24–26 Jul ET+TT
Two vaccines per animal per session System rule A hard limit on how many vaccines one animal can receive in a single session
Repeat cycles for PPR, pox, FMD, HS and adult Blue Tongue System rule The latest accepted dose of the same vaccine sets the next repeat. One vaccine is never anchored off another vaccine's dose, except for the medical spacing gaps above
Adult Blue Tongue booster at +3 weeks Farm instruction Asked for by the farm team, not part of the approved schedule today. The approved schedule carries Blue Tongue for kids at 16 and 20 weeks, and a yearly adult repeat from the accepted dose. Treat the 26 Aug and 31 Aug rows as farm-entered dates until an adult Blue Tongue second dose is configured
