Additional Vaccination Rules

* After procuring goats and keeping warming, even though they are vaccinated at the source, once it comes to warming, don't give any vaccination for a week  
* Spacing between two live vaccines must be 4 weeks  
* Can combine a bacterial and viral vaccine on the same day  
* Can also combine a live viral and killed viral vaccine on the same day  
* No need for vaccination for quarantine/ICU goats.

1\. Vaccine gap rules  
\- Live vaccine followed by killed vaccine: keep a 2-week gap.  
\- Killed vaccine followed by killed vaccine: keep a 2-week gap.  
\- Live vaccine followed by live vaccine: keep a 4 \-week gap.  
\- Any Booster is for kids after a 3-week gap.

2\. Pregnancy rule  
\- Goat/sheep pregnancy is around 5 months.  
\- Vaccines can be given up to 3 months of pregnancy.  
\- During the 4th and 5th months of pregnancy, vaccination can be skipped.  
\- After delivery, missed vaccines can be given within 2 weeks.

3\. Procurement/source vaccination rule  
\- When procuring adult goats/sheep, vaccination can be done at the source.  
\- Breeding and fattening goats/sheep can also be vaccinated at the source.  
\- For kids up to 16 weeks, the normal vaccination schedule must be followed properly.  
\- For adult procured animals, it is acceptable to vaccinate at the time of procurement.

4\. New animal procurement schedule  
\- For newly procured goats/sheep, give ET+TT \+ PPR first.  
\- Wait 4 weeks then give Goat Pox for goats or Sheep Pox for sheep \+ (ET+TT booster)
