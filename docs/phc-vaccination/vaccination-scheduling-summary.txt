PHC VACCINATION SCHEDULING — SUMMARY FOR REVIEW
=================================================

THE PROBLEM
-----------
We cannot pick a shed and vaccinate everyone who is "due."
Each goat/sheep needs the right vaccine, at the right time, in the right order.
Only after that can we group safe doses into a shed drive.


HOW IT WORKS (2 STEPS)
----------------------
STEP 1 — Per animal: "What dose does THIS animal need, and when?"
STEP 2 — Per shed: "Which of those doses can safely be done together on one day?"

Step 2 never runs without Step 1.


FIRST: CLASSIFY THE ANIMAL
--------------------------
Species     Goat or Sheep (different vaccines)
Path        Farm-born kid | Procured kid (≤16 weeks) | Procured adult
State       Normal | Warming (first 7 days) | ICU/Quarantine | Pregnant

Rules by path:
  Kids (born or procured young)  → full age-based schedule (strict)
  Adults (procured)              → shorter intake schedule at arrival
  Breeding/fattening adults      → can vaccinate at source/procurement


VACCINES
--------
GOATS:  ET+TT, PPR, Goat Pox, FMD, HS
SHEEP:  ET+TT, PPR, Blue Tongue, Sheep Pox, FMD, HS

Kid timing depends on: was the MOTHER vaccinated? (yes = later doses, no = earlier)


KID SCHEDULE (simplified)
-------------------------
GOAT KIDS — in order:
  1. ET+TT
  2. ET+TT booster (3 weeks later)
  3. PPR
  4. Goat Pox
  5. FMD + HS (same day OK)

SHEEP KIDS — in order:
  1. ET+TT
  2. ET+TT booster (3 weeks later)
  3. PPR + Blue Tongue (same day)
  4. Sheep Pox + Blue Tongue booster
  5. FMD + HS (same day OK)

Exact ages (weeks) vary by mother vaccinated or not — see Nuances Rules doc.


ADULT PROCUREMENT SCHEDULE
--------------------------
Day 0:     ET+TT + PPR (together)
Week 4:    Goat Pox (goats) or Sheep Pox (sheep) + ET+TT booster
Later:     FMD + HS on their repeat cycles

Adults do NOT follow the full kid week-by-week table.


GAP RULES (minimum wait between vaccines)
-----------------------------------------
Live  → Killed     2 weeks
Killed → Killed    2 weeks
Live  → Live       4 weeks
ET+TT → ET+TT booster (kids)     3 weeks
Procurement step 1 → step 2      4 weeks

CAN combine same day:
  - bacterial + viral
  - live viral + killed viral
  - named combos above (e.g. FMD + HS)

CANNOT combine same day:
  - two live vaccines if 4-week gap not met


OPERATIONAL RULES
-----------------
Warming (first 7 days after arrival)     No vaccination — wait
ICU / Quarantine                         No vaccination
Pregnancy months 1–3                     OK to vaccinate
Pregnancy months 4–5                   Skip — catch up within 2 weeks after delivery
Procured at source                     OK for adults; kids still need full schedule till 16 weeks


WHAT WE BUILD IN SOFTWARE
-------------------------
1. Rule engine per goat — creates "this animal owes dose X by date Y"
2. Gap checker — blocks doses too soon after last vaccine
3. State checker — warming, ICU, pregnancy blocks
4. Drive planner — only batches doses that are medically safe on the same day

We do NOT: schedule one drive for "everyone in the shed."


ALREADY BUILT vs STILL NEEDED
-----------------------------
Built:     per-goat due dates, booster chaining, basic shed grouping
Needed:    full vaccine matrix in config, live/killed gap enforcement,
