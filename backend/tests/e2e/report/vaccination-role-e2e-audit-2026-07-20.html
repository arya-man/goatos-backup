Legacy Slack provenance

The actual slack-automation-scripts repository contains no vaccination form/card schema and no source for cohort, route/site, administered-date, or adverse-reaction labels. The 17-page Slack Modules Training PDF also contains no vaccination form or relevant terms. Its only vaccination-specific helper is fixVaccinationAccess(), granting access to two opaque list IDs.

administered_at, adverse_reaction, and reaction_notes originate in the local source-material vaccination template cited by the Goat OS source-findings proposal. route_site is absent from Slack and approved vaccination rules; it came from a Goat OS draft SOP/general model. It means administration route/body site, but remains a source-provenance gap for product approval or removal. The visible cohort label was a Goat OS semantic mismatch and now uses the actual animal-stage meaning.
